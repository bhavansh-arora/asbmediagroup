(01) — The projectBrand anthem film

The brief

Show how a multidisciplinary team provides a safe, centralised environment for child abuse assessment, intervention and healing, and make the impact of that work felt.

The approach

Interview-led storytelling with the people who do the work, soft natural light and restrained, respectful framing. The building itself becomes a character: calm, safe and welcoming.

Built for

Donors, community partners and local families. One film that can open a fundraising gala, anchor the website and travel through partner networks.
