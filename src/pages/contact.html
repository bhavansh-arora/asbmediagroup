Good questions.

          
Can’t find your answer? Call or email and you’ll get a straight reply.

        

        

          
01How much does a commercial video cost?
Every project is quoted to its scope: length, shoot days, locations, crew and deliverables. After a short discovery call you’ll get a clear, fixed proposal with no hidden costs.

          
02How long does a brand film take?
Most commercial projects run three to six weeks from discovery to final delivery. Tighter deadlines are possible. Tell us your date and we’ll plan backwards from it.

          
03Where are you based, and do you travel?
We’re based in the Tampa Bay area and work throughout Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater and surrounding Florida locations. We’re also available to travel for commercial projects, weddings and special events.

          
04Will we get versions for social media?
Yes. We plan every shoot so the footage can be cut for 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, giving you assets for your website, Reels, TikTok, YouTube and paid ads from one production.

          
05How do we reserve a wedding date?
A signed contract and a non-refundable deposit secure your date. Payment plans are available for the balance. We recommend booking several months in advance, as popular dates fill quickly.

          
06When will we receive our photos or film?
Wedding galleries typically take a few weeks to fully edit and deliver. Commercial timelines are agreed in your proposal and always include time for revisions.

          
07What types of photography do you offer?
Photography and videography for weddings, engagements, commercial projects, sports and products, either on its own or alongside a film shoot.
